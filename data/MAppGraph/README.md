> ⚠️ **Full dataset gated (email request).** The complete ~58.5 GB of PCAPs is **not** public —
> the authors share it via Google Drive "to specific email if requested (only for academic
> research purposes)." Only small CSV feature samples are on GitHub.

**Source / project site:** https://soeai.github.io/mappgraph/ · Repo: https://github.com/soeai/mappgraph
**Sample data (public, on GitHub):** `DATA T5_3` and `DATA T2_0` folders —
https://github.com/soeai/MAppGraph/tree/gh-pages/mappgraph/data
(CSV: `features.csv`, `weights.csv`, for T_window = 5 min and 2 min)
**Request full dataset:** email dung.cao@ttu.edu.vn and truonghuu.tram@singaporetech.edu.sg
(state academic research purpose).

**Paper:** Pham, T.D., Ho, T.L., Truong-Huu, T., Cao, T.D., Truong, H.L., "MAppGraph:
Mobile-App Classification on Encrypted Network Traffic using Deep Graph Convolution Neural
Networks," ACSAC 2021. DOI: 10.1145/3485832.3485925

## What it is
Encrypted network traffic for **~100 Android mobile apps**, used to build per-app traffic
graphs for GCN-based app classification.

## Used by
- "Generalized Encrypted Traffic Classification Using Inter-Flow Signals," arXiv:2508.21558v1
  — mobile-app fingerprinting.

## After obtaining
Place the PCAPs (or CSV features) here and record the app list, T_window variants, and sizes.
